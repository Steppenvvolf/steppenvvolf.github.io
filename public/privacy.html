Privacy Policy

This policy covers the private backup tools run by Stefan Malinovic, including the "rclone" Google OAuth app.

These tools are used only by their owner, on his own computer, with his own Google account. They are not offered to the public.

  • Google Drive access is limited to the files the tool itself creates (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other files.
  • No personal data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Access tokens stay on the owner's computer.

Questions: see the contact details on steppenvvolf.github.io.

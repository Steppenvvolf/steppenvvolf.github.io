These identifications concern what appears in the images. They do not establish who created the asset, which work Stefan personally performed, whether a client/studio permits public use, or whether the image is current. Confirm those points against original media and approved project records before publishing. When evidence is missing, leave the image slot empty and use the designed typography treatment.
